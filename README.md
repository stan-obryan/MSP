Problem:
Many small businesses heavily rely on computers and other technology to conduct business every day. Hiring someone fulltime is very costly for small businesses so Managed Service Providers allow small businesses and teams to get enterprise level IT services for a fraction of the cost.

Value:
Enterprise level IT services for even the smallest of organizations.

Project Plan

Ideally the whole project plan would be to develop an entire functional website but the goal here was to build a tool that delivers value quickly so a landing page with this quiz was the ideal route to take. This is something I eventually want to start up so here is a small building block in that process.
The smallest demonstration of value that I could identify is a risk assessment quiz that takes two minutes to fill out that quickly identifies how valueable outsourcing IT services to industry experts for a fraction of the cost of hiring someone full time to perform the tasks is necessary to protect their digital and physical (data) assets and keep them secure. Its free and zero risk and collects email addresses to begin building a client database.
